# CASE FILE — The Far Corner
> The player's earned knowledge. Recall questions are answered ONLY from here.
> Everything below was legitimately uncovered through investigation.

## The opening

You are a detective with the **Knoxville Police Department, Major Crimes Unit**.
At ~6:45 AM on a Tuesday in July you're called to the **north parking lot of West
Town Mall** (7600 Kingston Pike, West Knoxville).

## Established facts

- A **silver Honda CR-V** is backed into a far corner of the lot, toward Morrell
  Road, well away from the mall doors and poorly covered by cameras.
- The **driver's-side window is smashed**. A woman in her early 40s is slumped
  dead in the driver's seat with evident **head trauma**.
- The victim is identified from the vehicle registration as **Dana Kessler, 41**,
  of West Knoxville.
- She was found at **~6:20 AM by Lorraine Petty**, a retired teacher who walks the
  lot most mornings before the mall opens; Ms. Petty flagged mall security, who
  called 911.
- Her **purse appears to be missing**. Patrol's initial read is a **robbery /
  carjacking**, and dispatch noted a recent string of **car break-ins** at the
  mall lot.
- The **mall closed at 9:00 PM**. It was a warm, dry July night.

## Open threads

- Why was Dana at the mall lot after closing, in a remote corner?
- Who is she — family, work, who saw her last? Next-of-kin notification.
- Autopsy / cause and time of death (Knox County Regional Forensic Center).
- Scene forensics: the smashed glass, the "robbery," anything left behind.
- The missing purse; the robbery theory and the recent break-ins.
