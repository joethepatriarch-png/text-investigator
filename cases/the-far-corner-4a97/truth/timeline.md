# TIMELINE (true) — The Far Corner
> 🔒 FIREWALL — the real sequence of events. Private.

Anchor: the murder is on a **Monday night**; the body is found **Tuesday**
morning (the opening). In-game "today" at the opening is that Tuesday.

## Background (the slow burn)
- ~22 years ago: Dana and Cole's mother dies. Dana (19) raises Cole (12). The
  defining fact of both their lives.
- ~4 years ago: Dana gets Cole the dispatcher/parts job at Preston Heating & Air.
- ~2 years ago: Dana divorces Greg Marsh; takes back "Kessler"; keeps son Aidan.
- ~14 months ago: Cole's sports-betting losses spiral (TN online books legal since
  2020); he goes past app limits to a private bookie, **Marlon Petro**.
- ~8 months ago: Cole sets up shell vendor **"Summit Mechanical Supply"** and begins
  skimming — fake POs, off-book cash jobs — to service the debt.

## The two weeks before
- ~14 days before: Dana, doing the quarterly reconciliation, finds the "Summit"
  payments with no deliveries and the cash gaps. Traces access to Cole. Confronts him
  privately. Cole breaks down, invents **Sophie's leukemia / out-of-network bills**.
  Dana believes it utterly, agrees to quietly cover the hole and give him a chance to
  stop. Tells no one.
- ~3 days before: Dana finds a **fresh** fake "Summit" invoice dated after their talk.
  He didn't stop. She's now frightened — for Hal, for herself as the bookkeeper, and
  starting to doubt Cole. She updates her backup notes at home.
- The night before (Sunday): Dana writes in her backup notes the line
  *"whatever it takes, we keep Sophie's family together — one last time,"* and resolves
  to make Cole either repay on a real schedule or go to Hal with her.

## Monday — the day of
- Daytime: normal work day at Preston. Dana quiet, tense; Cole normal on the surface.
  Denny Preston is already in Nashville for a trade show. Hal notices nothing new.
- ~7:00 PM: Dana home, makes dinner, seems preoccupied to Aidan.
- ~9:45 PM: Dana calls Cole (or he calls her); they agree to meet — she names the West
  Town Mall north lot, ~10:15, to talk it out away from Brooke and work. (This call
  survives in carrier records; Aidan half-hears her end.)
- ~9:50–10:00 PM: Dana tells Aidan she's "running out to deal with something about Uncle
  Cole and work — lock up, I'll be back." Leaves in her silver CR-V.
- ~10:10 PM: Dana arrives, backs into the far north-lot corner toward Morrell Road,
  engine off, waits.
- ~10:12 PM: Cole arrives in his truck, parks a row over, gets into Dana's passenger
  seat. He's brought a heavy aluminum flashlight from the truck.
- ~10:15–10:30 PM: They argue. Dana: come clean to Hal or I will; I won't go to prison
  for you. Cole panics — exposure means prison, losing his family, and the sick-child
  lie unravelling. He strikes her several times on the left side of the head with the
  flashlight. She dies in the driver's seat. (TOD ~10–11 PM.)
- ~10:30–10:40 PM: Cole stages it. Out of the car; smashes the driver's window from
  outside with the flashlight; reaches in; takes her purse (and realises it holds her
  discrepancy notebook). Leaves rings + console cash. Knocks her phone down between seat
  and console without noticing. Wipes the passenger door. Back to his truck.
- ~10:45 PM: Cole leaves the lot. His phone has pinged the tower covering West Town Mall
  ~10:15–10:45; his truck passes an LPR / a business camera on the Kingston Pike /
  Morrell Road corridor.
- ~11:00 PM: Detour to the **Northshore Drive bridge**; throws the flashlight, purse, and
  notebook into the water.
- ~11:35 PM: Home. Tells Brooke (half-asleep) he was "out with a buddy." Brooke will later,
  from love, say he was in bed.

## Tuesday — discovery (the opening)
- ~6:20 AM: **Lorraine Petty**, a morning mall-walker, sees the CR-V backed into the far
  corner, window smashed, a woman slumped inside. Flags mall security → 911.
- ~6:35 AM: KPD patrol arrives; sees smashed window + missing purse; dispatch notes the
  recent mall car break-ins → early read "robbery/carjacking."
- ~6:45 AM: **You**, KPD Major Crimes, are called to the scene. Vehicle registration IDs
  the victim as **Dana Kessler, 41**, of West Knoxville. The game begins.

## Where everyone is on Monday night (for alibis)
- **Cole**: at the mall killing Dana ~10:12–10:45, then the bridge, then home ~11:35.
  Claims home-by-9 / "out with a buddy." No real cover for 10–11 PM.
- **Brooke**: home, asleep by ~10:30; will soft-cover Cole.
- **Aidan**: home, gaming; heard his mom leave ~9:55 re: "Uncle Cole and work."
- **Greg Marsh** (ex): Bearden bar from ~8, then home with girlfriend Tara — camera +
  receipt + Tara cover ~9:30 to morning. Didn't know about the mall.
- **Denny Preston**: Nashville trade show; hotel + registration + colleagues cover him.
- **Denny Ray Hobbs** (prowler): in Knox County jail (booked 2 days earlier). Hard alibi.
- **Hal Preston**: home with his wife; not a suspect.
- **Marlon Petro** (bookie): irrelevant to the night; surfaces later via the money.
