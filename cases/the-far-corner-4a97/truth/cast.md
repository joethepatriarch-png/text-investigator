# CAST & LOCATIONS — The Far Corner
> 🔒 FIREWALL — private. `revealed:` flags track what the player has earned.

## People

### Dana Kessler — victim, 41
Bookkeeper / office manager at Preston Heating & Air for 12 years. Divorced from
Greg Marsh; son Aidan, 15, lives with her. Went back to her maiden name (Kessler)
after the divorce. Raised her younger brother Cole after their mother died when
Dana was 19 and Cole was 12 — she is, in every way that matters, the parent who
brought him up. Meticulous, loyal, trusted absolutely by old Hal Preston.
KNOWS (in life): the whole embezzlement, that it's Cole, the sick-child story she
believed, and that she'd arranged to meet him at the mall. All of this is now for
the investigator to reconstruct.
- revealed: partial (identity, that she's the victim — known from the opening)

### Cole Kessler — the killer, 34
Dana's younger brother. Dispatcher / parts manager at Preston Heating & Air (Dana
got him the job ~4 years ago). Married to Brooke; daughter Sophie, 6. Warm,
well-liked, "the fun uncle." Underneath: deep in sports-betting debt (Tennessee
online books) and worse to a private bookie, **Marlon Petro**. Set up the shell
vendor "Summit Mechanical Supply" and skimmed via fake invoices + off-book cash
jobs. Told Dana the money was for Sophie's (invented) leukemia — the lie that made
his sister protect him. Killed Dana at the mall when she pushed him to come clean;
staged the robbery; dumped purse/notebook/flashlight off the Northshore Drive
bridge.
WILL SAY: grief; that he loved his sister; that he has no idea who'd do this;
initially that he was home / "out with a buddy" the night of. If pressed on money,
he'll deny, then (cornered) fall back on the sick-child story as justification.
WILL HIDE: the theft, the gambling, that Sophie was never sick, that he met Dana at
the mall, that he owns/owned a heavy aluminum flashlight, his true whereabouts.
LIES: extensively. Alibi, motive, and the sick-child story are all lies.
Tells: over-eager to push the robbery/prowler theory; asks what you've found rather
than what happened; flinches at "Summit Mechanical," at Sophie's health being
checked, and at his phone being pulled.
- revealed: no

### Brooke Kessler — Cole's wife, 32
Dental hygienist. Loves Cole; does not know he's a killer and does not fully know
the gambling — she thinks money's been "tight because work's slow." Believes, or
wants to believe, Cole was home the night of the murder; she was asleep by ~10:30
and will initially say he was in bed. **Does NOT know any sick-child story** — asked
plainly about Sophie's health, she's baffled: Sophie has never been seriously ill.
That honest bafflement is the pin that pops Cole's motive lie.
WILL SAY: Cole loved Dana; the family's had money stress; Sophie is a healthy,
normal kid.
WILL HIDE: nothing knowingly — but she'll shade the alibi toward Cole out of
loyalty until confronted with his real whereabouts.
LIES: only the soft alibi, and only from love, not knowledge.
- revealed: no

### Sophie Kessler — Cole & Brooke's daughter, 6
Healthy. Never had leukemia or any serious illness. Exists in the case only as the
records check that detonates the motive lie (pediatric/oncology history is clean).
- revealed: no

### Aidan Kessler — Dana's son, 15
Grieving, shaken, honest. Was home gaming in his room the night of; heard his mom
take/make a call around 9:45–10, sounded stressed, and tell him she was "running out
to deal with something about Uncle Cole and work — lock up, I'll be back." He assumed
she came home; woke to police. Doesn't know she went to the mall. Handle gently (§9).
KNOWS: she left late, upset, re: "Uncle Cole and work," after a call. That's it.
- revealed: no

### Hal Preston — owner, Preston Heating & Air, 66
Founder, old-school, honest. Loved Dana like a daughter; naively fond of Cole.
Doesn't suspect embezzlement — but recalls Dana seeming worried the past month about
"a mistake in the books" she wanted to run down before bothering him. Will grant
access to company books once persuaded or on a warrant. Devastated. A key
victimology + document source; not a suspect.
KNOWS: Dana was the most honest person he knew; she'd flagged "a mistake in the
books"; Cole works dispatch/parts and handles vendor POs and cash-job intake.
- revealed: no

### Denny Preston — Hal's son, 39 (RED HERRING)
Works "in the business" in a vague VP capacity; wants to modernise, cut costs, and
ease his father out. Resented Dana's authority and her closeness with Hal; they
clashed, and staff will report friction. Was at a Nashville HVAC trade show the
night of the murder (hotel + registration + colleagues verify). No motive to kill
Dana; may push the robbery theory to keep eyes off the company. Distraction, not
culprit.
- revealed: no

### Greg Marsh — Dana's ex-husband, 44 (RED HERRING)
Bitter divorce two years ago; behind on child support; a prior for assault (bar
fight) and a DUI. Temper, and no love lost. Looks perfect early. But: he was at
**Sassy Ann's / a Bearden bar** drinking that night, then went home with his
girlfriend Tara — bar camera, a card receipt, and Tara's statement cover ~9:30 PM
to morning. He also had no way to know Dana would be at the mall. Clears on
checking. If leaned on wrongly, he lawyers up and the misstep costs time.
- revealed: no

### Marlon Petro — private bookie, 40s
The man Cole owes past the app limits. A name that surfaces from Cole's finances
(cash withdrawals, a couple of traceable transfers) once the money trail opens.
Not the killer; won't talk without leverage; corroborates the *gambling* truth, not
a *medical* one. Confirms the real hole in Cole's story.
- revealed: no

### Lorraine Petty — witness who found the body, 72
Retired teacher, walks the mall lot loops most mornings before it opens. ~6:20 AM
saw the silver CR-V backed into the far corner with the driver's window smashed and
a woman slumped inside; flagged mall security, who called 911. Saw no one, no other
car of note. Honest, rattled, no further info. Discovery witness only.
- revealed: partial (referenced in the opening as the person who found the car)

### "The mall prowler" — Denny Ray Hobbs, 27 (RED HERRING PERSON)
KPD's favourite for a recent string of car break-ins at West Town Mall and Turkey
Creek: smash a window on an unlocked/soft car in daylight, grab visible valuables,
gone in 90 seconds. Nonviolent. **Was in the Knox County jail on the night of the
murder** (booked two days earlier on a separate charge) — a hard alibi. Chasing him
resolves the robbery theory as a dead end.
- revealed: no

## Locations

### West Town Mall — north parking lot, far corner (toward Morrell Road)
Primary scene. 7600 Kingston Pike, Knoxville — inside city limits → **Knoxville
Police Department** jurisdiction (Major Crimes). Silver Honda CR-V backed into a
remote corner spot, poorly covered by cameras (part of why it read as robbery).
Driver's window smashed **from outside, after death**. Evidence: staging (glass
under the shards on the seat, no glass in wounds), rings + console cash left, purse
gone, phone knocked between seat and console (recoverable → carrier + contacts),
touch DNA/partial on passenger interior, wound geometry from the passenger side.
- revealed: partial (the scene as visible at the opening)

### Preston Heating & Air — Kingston Pike, Bearden
The company. Books here hold the embezzlement: "Summit Mechanical Supply" POs with
no matching deliveries, cash-job intake gaps, an approval/access trail to Cole.
Hal's office; Cole's dispatch desk; the shared server. Access needs Hal's consent
or a warrant.
- revealed: no

### Dana's house — West Hills, West Knoxville
Where Aidan is. Dana's home computer / a thumb drive / emails-to-herself hold her
backup reconciliation notes (the trail duplicating the stolen notebook), including
the "one last time / keep Sophie's family together" line. Her calendar/notes hint at
a late "meeting." Needs consent (next of kin) or a warrant, both easily obtained.
- revealed: no

### "Summit Mechanical Supply" — the shell vendor
Not a real business. Registered address is a UPS Store mailbox / vacant unit;
bank account routes to Cole. The paper thread that names him.
- revealed: no

### Northshore Drive bridge (over the river)
Where Cole dumped the purse, notebook, and flashlight the night of. Physical
recovery is possible but not guaranteed — a dive/search is a lead check (§4), not a
gimme. A HIT here yields the weapon and/or the notebook (nail-in-the-coffin
forensics + Dana's own words).
- revealed: no

### Knox County Regional Forensic Center — Middlebrook Pike
Where the autopsy is done (fictional ME **Dr. Priya Nair**). Findings: blunt-force
head trauma, multiple blows, heavy cylindrical object; blows from the passenger
side; window broken post-mortem; TOD ~10–11 PM. Toxicology pending (comes back
unremarkable). Forensic authority for the case.
- revealed: no
