# TIMELINE (true) — Blind Corner
> 🔒 FIREWALL — the real sequence of events. Private.

## Deep background
- **2016** — Harold Cate dies. Leaves Nell the Sequoyah Hills house (already
  reverse-mortgaged in later years to fund care) and his restored **1966 Ford
  Mustang**, kept in a storage unit off Middlebrook Pike.
- **~2023 →** — Brant's HVAC business (Cate Mechanical) begins failing.

## 2026 — the slide
- **Feb** — Nell diagnosed, pancreatic cancer; goes on home hospice. Cumberland
  Home Care assigns **Paula Hutchens**.
- **Feb–Jun** — Paula nurses Nell; a real bond forms. Brant visits rarely, mostly
  about money/the house; friction with Paula, whom he casts as an interloper.
  Brant sinks: tax liens, foreclosure filed (May), divorce filed (June), gambling
  losses at Harrah's Cherokee.
- **~May** — Nell, lucid and grateful, has attorney **Doug Sharp** draft a
  **codicil**: the Mustang + $10,000 to Paula, with a handwritten note asking Paula
  to pass the car to granddaughter **Sadie** when grown. Nell tells Brant she's
  "taken care of that sweet nurse." Brant hears *the nurse is in the will* and
  spirals.
- **~Jun 28** — Nell dies at home; Paula present, grieves genuinely.
- **Late Jun–mid Jul** — Probate begins; Brant learns the estate is a shell
  (reverse mortgage) but fixates that Paula "got the good stuff" and cheated him.
  He starts calling/texting Paula — demands escalate to threats. Paula, unsettled,
  mentions it to Renae and to Maddie.

## Sunday, July 19, 2026 — the killing
- **Daytime** — Ordinary Bearden Sunday for Paula; Maddie is at her dad Rick's for
  the weekend, so Paula is alone that night (why no one flags her missing).
- **~7:00–9:00pm** — Brant texts/calls Paula: wants to "clear the air about Mom,"
  apologize; says he's embarrassed, doesn't want a scene at her home or in public.
  Proposes the quiet mall overflow lot, "back by the old Sears," ~10pm. Paula
  agrees, hoping to defuse him.
- **~9:45pm** — Brant drives his white F-250 east on Kingston Pike to West Town
  Mall; parks in the dark SW overflow lot. (Cell tower ping begins; truck caught
  on a Kingston Pike camera.)
- **~10:15pm** — Paula arrives in her silver CR-V, parks nose-in facing the tree
  line. Brant gets into her passenger seat.
- **~10:15–10:30pm** — Talk curdles fast: he accuses her of manipulating his mother
  and stealing his inheritance. Paula tries to explain the estate is insolvent and
  the car is meant for *his own daughter Sadie* — which enrages him further.
- **~10:30pm** — He pushes her back against the headrest and smothers her with a
  soft item (his work jacket / a moving blanket from the truck). She dies. (Bruised
  lip/gum, occipital contusion, conjunctival petechiae.)
- **~10:40pm** — He takes her purse (with her phone) to fake a robbery and erase
  the text thread; leaves her jewelry and the car; wipes what he thinks to; the
  driver's window is down ~2 in. Drives off west.
- **~10:50–11:15pm** — Truck caught leaving on Kingston Pike; tower ping fades
  toward Farragut. En route he ditches the purse/phone in a dumpster (or retention
  pond) behind a Kingston Pike strip center; the phone last-locates near the mall
  before going dark.
- **Overnight** — Brant reaches his Farragut house. Kelli knows he was out and that
  he later lied about it.

## Monday, July 20, 2026 — discovery
- **~6:15am** — Landscaping foreman **Hector Villalobos**, mowing the mall islands
  at dawn, notices the lone CR-V with a still figure inside; calls 911.
- **~6:30am** — **Ofc. Tyisha Boyd (KPD)** responds; runs the plate → registered to
  **Paula A. Hutchens**, Bearden. Purse/phone missing; jewelry on; car untaken.
  Major Crimes summoned. **← OPENING SCENE (the player arrives).**

- Cumberland Home Care → Renae, the Cate case, the harassment, the Mustang-for-Sadie

- Doug Sharp → codicil + note + insolvent estate.
- Public records → Brant's ruin. Tower + Kingston Pike cameras → break his alibi.
